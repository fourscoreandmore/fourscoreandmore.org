# Apps in your browser

A trial of our apps running in your browser, with [Pyodide](https://pyodide.org/),
rather than on our server. They use the same code as the server versions, and
should give the same results.

The first time you use one, your browser downloads Python and
[music21](https://www.music21.org/) (about 12 MB), so it takes a little while
to start. After that it's quicker.

- [Chorale Exercises](chorales/) (server version: [/apps/chorales](/apps/chorales/))
- [Lieder Exercises](lieder/) (server version: [/apps/lieder](/apps/lieder/))
- [Working in Harmony](working-in-harmony/) (server version: [/apps/working-in-harmony](/apps/working-in-harmony/))
