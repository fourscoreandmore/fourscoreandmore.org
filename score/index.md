At the heart of fourscoreandmore is the musical "score"
(aka "symbolic data" aka "sheet music").
In our case, the focus is on digital encodings of scores such that
we can make use of helpful modern functionality.
One key priority of fourscoreandmore
has been to
make more digital scores,
by more composers,
more easily accessible.

In the spirit of open scores, this site serves to provide
easy overviews and direct download to individual files
without any login and certainly without payment.
It's hard to be more open than that!

This page lists all (known) score collections meeting the following (strict!) criteria: 
- Digitally encoded scores in a standard format (MusicXML, MuseScore, MEI) 🎼 🎵
- Clearly stated, maximally open CC0 or PD licence (supporting all use cases) ⛓️‍💥
- Free to download individual files or the whole collection, with no login required or prospect of a corporate takeover ⛓️‍💥
- Proper provenance information available ℹ️
- Detailed editorial notes 📝

If that sounds good, you came to the right place!

Have we missed a dataset meting the above criteria? Do you have one?
If so, let us know and we'll include it!

## Primarily Pedagogical Texts
- Species Counterpoint:
  - [About the Species Corpus](../species). Also provided (via that page):
    - Whole Part (I, II, III) Direct download in multiple formats.
    - [Search, sort, and direct download individual figures](../species/contents)
      - Download any in either mxl or krn format
      - View and interact with online using Verovio Humdrum Viewer (VHV)
    - For reading and engaging the book in full, fourscore recommends:
    [playablebooks](https://playablebook.com/fux-counterpoint/).
