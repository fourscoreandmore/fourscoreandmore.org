# fourscoreandmore.org

A Jekyll site, plus a Flask app in `apps-backend/` (see its README for tests).

When changing how scores are displayed (the OpenSheetMusicDisplay version in
`apps-backend/package.json`, or `apps-backend/app/templates/display-music.html`),
render scores before and after and compare the screenshots: see "Checking score
rendering" in `apps-backend/README.md`.
