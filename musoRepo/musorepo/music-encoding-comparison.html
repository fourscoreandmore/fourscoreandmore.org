How to read the badges

Direct

A documented construct or application workflow expresses this feature within the stated scope.

Qualified

Representable, but a significant semantic, structural, profile or implementation condition applies.

Extension

Requires a named dialect, adjacent representation or agreed additional vocabulary.

Workaround

Approximate, reframe or externalise the information; some semantics or convenience are lost.

Unverified

The reviewed evidence does not establish the specific capability; a targeted test is needed.

Evidence boundary: capability judgements are based on documentation or labelled inference. No converter round trips or playback tests were run. “Direct” is not a guarantee that a particular program supports the feature.

Worked comparison

An editorial sharp with a responsible editor

A displayed bracket is only the beginning.

Suppose a manuscript has an F, and an editor proposes F-sharp for performance. Preserve the proposed pitch, the absence of the accidental in the source, who supplied it, and the reason. Do not confuse an editorial sharp with a courtesy sharp.

  1. KRN: in a VHV profile, declare an RDF editorial-accidental signifier. Add a separate responsibility/comment convention if that information must be queryable.
  2. MusicXML: encode the altered pitch and an editorial accidental; level/footnote can explain the act. A witness-based apparatus requires additional conventions or external data.
  3. MuseScore: create the intended notation and visible editorial treatment. Preserve responsibility and evidence in an apparatus instead of expecting the bracket to carry them.
  4. MEI: retain the supplied accidental and attribution, and use choice or app when the original and alternative reading must both remain available.

Voice identity is separate from chord membership and stem direction.

Take one 4/4 bar with a whole-note bass and four quarter notes above it. The sustained bass and moving line must retain independent durations and voice identity.

Worked comparison

3/4 against 4/4, at the same pulse

Independent bar boundaries, not just different printed signatures.

On a common quarter-note timeline, the 3/4 part has boundaries at 0, 3, 6, 9, 12; the 4/4 part at 0, 4, 8, 12. Their first common endpoint is 12. Contrast this with differently written metres whose entire bars are stretched to the same duration.

  1. KRN/VHV: use the union of boundary times, with invisible bar markers in the staff that does not actually start a new bar. Count local measures independently.
  2. MusicXML: partwise non-controlling measures are designed for non-aligned left boundaries. Test whether the destination preserves them or forces a shared bar grid.
  3. MuseScore: local signatures rescale to the global bar span. A common-grid workaround can produce the picture, but it changes structural measures and may affect numbering, rests, repeats and export.
  4. MEI: preserve non-controlling barlines in a suitable part-oriented or unmeasured organisation. Do not assume that marking an ordinary shared measure as non-controlling automatically creates independent local bar containers. On a measure, MEI control describes the right boundary; MusicXML non-controlling describes the left.

Scope, evidence and reproducibility

The comparison focuses on symbolic music representation, with light treatment of shared basics. Research date: 2026-11-06.

  • KRN means Humdrum **kern, not every representation that can inhabit a Humdrum file. Adjacent representations and VHV conventions are labelled.
  • MusicXML means the published 4.0 specification; a receiving application may implement much less.
  • MuseScore 4 means the native MSCZ/MSCX project and the application workflows documented for the 4.x family. Exact release differences are retained where known. This is not a frozen source-code audit of one build.
  • MEI means 5.1 across the full framework; MEI Basic and repertoire-specific customizations can exclude features.
  • Direct is not a quality score or a promise of render/playback/import support. No format is assigned an overall winner.

Maintaining this report (access to underpinning data files required)

Edit comparison.json and run python3 build.py. The script validates identifiers, references and required fields, then embeds the data in this standalone HTML. It uses only Python’s standard library. The source package includes the template, a JSON Schema and a focused verification script.

JSON was chosen over YAML to avoid parser dependencies and implicit typing surprises. Capability, evidence, profile and empirical-test status are separate fields. Filters do not treat a workaround as direct semantic support.
