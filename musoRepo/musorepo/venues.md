Journals, conferences and trade fairs relevant to music computing: for reading around the topic and,
for newcomers, for ideas of where to submit work. “Link rot” is a well-known and persistent problem.
Links on this page are checked automatically every week, but change is constant,
so 100% accuracy is impossible.

Names highlight a keyword (**bold**) that is used for sorting. Words common to many titles
(“International”, “Conference”, regions like “Australasian” or “European”, and publishers like “ACM” and “IEEE”)
are de-emphasised. We do not distinguish journals from conferences: in computer science the difference is slight,
as most conferences have extensive peer review and full written proceedings.
