About the analysis and keyboard controls

This opening passage is a double canon in inversion. The dux is the leading voice; the comes follows. Keys 1–4 trace the four voices across instruments. Keys 5–9 compare five paired blocks in the first canon, retaining its red and green colours. Selecting a pair opens its first page if none of it is on the current page. The bar ranges and page locations appear below the controls; some pairs continue onto another page.

Key 0 highlights the sounding pitch classes A and E♭, including their spellings in the transposing instruments (all notes shown here at sounding pitch). This part of the analysis draws attention to the pitch symmetry around (sounding) A3.

Use ←/→ for pages, Ctrl + ←/Ctrl + → for first/last page, +/− for zoom, and Esc to clear highlighting. On a narrow screen, scroll the score sideways.
